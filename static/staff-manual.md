# Beamers Farm Staff Digital Wiki

## Purpose
This manual covers order handling, payment verification, cancellation reasons, customer privacy, staff profiles, reports, and escalation.

## Core rule
Protect customer data, verify money before confirmation, leave accurate records, and escalate high-impact exceptions to the Owner.

## Customer accounts
Use the Status field: Verified, Unverified, or Private Community Profile. Never copy private details outside the staff portal.

## Payment
Confirm only after the exact amount is visible in the business POS or bank account. Screenshots are not payment proof.

## Cancellation
Select and retain the factual cancellation reason. It appears in Order Summary reports.

## Staff profile
Salespeople may update their phone, email, socials, and address one field at a time from Actions > Staff profile.

## Reports
Check timeframe, filters, status counts, and customer visibility before printing or saving as PDF.

## Escalation
Escalate payment mismatches, privacy incidents, account compromise, stock discrepancies, refund promises, and delivery exceptions to the Owner.
