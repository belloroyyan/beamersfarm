How to get to the farm

Address
Elapop Estate, Coker Omu Community,
along UNIOSUN Main Campus Road,
Oke Baale, Osogbo, Osun State.

Nearby landmark: Barracks, along Boredun–Egbeda Road, Osogbo.

  1. Use the Barracks on Boredun–Egbeda Road as a landmark when asking for directions to Oke Baale.
  2. From Sasa Market, a local rickshaw to Oke Baale is approximately ₦400. Motorcycle (bike) riders are approximately ₦500–₦600.
  3. At the entrance to Elapop Estate, look for the Beamers Farm banner. Once inside the estate, walk straight ahead; the path leads to the farm.

Transport fares are approximate and may vary. Please confirm the fare with your rider before setting off.

Operating hours7:00 AM–7:00 PM

02

Browse

Choose your chicken

  1. Open Products in the navigation or browse the popular picks on the home page.
  2. Open a product to see its details, price, pack size, and availability.
  3. Select a quantity and choose Add to crate. The crate count in the header updates as you shop.
03

Review

Check your crate

  1. Open Crate to review your chosen items, quantities, subtotal, and delivery fee.
  2. Change a quantity and press Update crate, or use the × control to remove an item.
  3. When your order looks right, choose Continue to delivery.
Open your crate →
04

Place an order

Enter delivery details

  1. Enter the name and phone number the farm should use to contact you.
  2. Provide a complete delivery address in Osogbo, including house number, street, and area.
  3. The WhatsApp order-update checkbox is optional. Submit the order when your details are ready.

Delivery is within Osogbo. If the shop is closed for orders, you can still browse the site and return when ordering reopens.

05

After checkout

Keep your order reference

  1. After submitting, the confirmation page shows your order number, items, delivery address, and total.
  2. Use the order number as the bank-transfer reference. Wait for Beamers Farm to confirm your order and provide payment instructions before transferring.
  3. Save the confirmation page. If you contact the farm about your order, include the reference number so the team can find it quickly.
