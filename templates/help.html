How to get to the farm

Address
Elapop Estate, Coker Omu Community,
along UNIOSUN Main Campus Road,
Oke Baale, Osogbo, Osun State.

Nearby landmark: Barracks, along Boredun–Egbeda Road, Osogbo.

  1. Use the Barracks on Boredun–Egbeda Road as a landmark when asking for directions to Oke Baale.
  2. From Sasa Market, a local rickshaw to Elapop Estate is approximately ₦400. Motorcycle (bike) riders to Elapop Estate are approximately ₦500–₦600.
  3. At the entrance to Elapop Estate, look for the Beamers Farm banner. Once inside the estate, walk straight ahead; the path leads to the farm.

Transport fares are approximate and may vary. Please confirm the fare with your rider before setting off.

Operating hours7:00 AM–7:00 PM

Need help finding us? Call 0806 207 4302.

02

Browse

Choose your chicken

  1. Open Products in the navigation or browse the popular picks on the home page.
  2. Open a product to see its details, price, pack size, and availability.
  3. Select a quantity and choose Add to cart. The cart count in the header updates as you shop.
03

Review

Check your cart

  1. Open Cart to review your items, quantities, and subtotal. The delivery fee is calculated after you select a zone at checkout.
  2. Change a quantity and press Update cart, or use the × control to remove an item.
  3. When your order looks right, continue to checkout to choose delivery or free farm pickup.
Open your cart →
04

Place an order

Enter delivery details

  1. Enter the name and phone number the farm should use to contact you.
  2. Choose a delivery zone and enter a complete address in that area, including house number, street, and neighborhood. Or choose free pickup from Beamers Farm.
  3. The WhatsApp order-update checkbox is optional. Submit the order when your details are ready.

Available delivery zones and their current fees are shown at checkout. Please ensure your selected dropdown zone matches your typed address to avoid order cancellation or extra cash-on-delivery charges. Farm pickup is free from Elapop Estate, 7:00 AM–7:00 PM. If the shop is closed for orders, you can still browse the site and return when ordering reopens.

05

After checkout

Keep your order reference

  1. Choose the quantity you need for each product. Every current product uses its listed price multiplied by the quantity ordered.
  2. After submitting, the order page shows your order number, items, fulfillment choice, complete amount due, and bank-transfer details. You can open and print a customer receipt from there.
  3. Transfer the complete amount before your order is confirmed. Use the order number as the bank-transfer reference. Beamers Farm checks its business bank/POS account and confirms only after the payment appears.
  4. Keep the order reference so the farm can find your order quickly. You can optionally enable browser notifications on the receipt page to hear when payment is verified and your order is confirmed.
