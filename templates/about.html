Who we are

A local farm with a simple promise.

In 2026, Beamers Farms was started by the Bellos, a family with experience in food and poultry. We wanted to make it easier for people in Osogbo to access fresh, clean, hygienically packaged chicken and reliable poultry products.

Our goal has always been to provide products that are reliable, fairly priced, and convenient. We understand the needs of local households, families, restaurants, and businesses because we are part of the same community.

That local connection influences how we package our products, communicate with customers, manage deliveries, and continue improving the business.
