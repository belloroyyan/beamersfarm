About Beamers Farms

Started by the Bellos, a family with experience in food and poultry, to make fresh, clean, hygienically packaged chicken easier to get in Osogbo.

Who we are

In 2026, Beamers Farms was started by the Bellos, a family with experience in food and poultry. We wanted to make it easier for people in Osogbo to access fresh, clean, hygienically packaged chicken and reliable poultry products.

Our goal has always been to provide products that are reliable, fairly priced, and convenient. Beamers Farms is based in Oke Baale, Osogbo, and we are proud to serve customers in Osogbo.

We understand the needs of local households, families, restaurants, and businesses because we are part of the same community. That local connection influences how we package our products, communicate with customers, manage deliveries, and continue improving the business.

What makes us different

Beamers Farms stands out among local poultry sellers in Osogbo by combining farm-to-table production with a modern e-commerce ordering experience. While many traditional poultry farms require buyers to visit in person, bargain over live birds, or handle slaughtering and cleaning themselves, Beamers Farms offers ready-to-cook frozen portions with transparent pricing and zone-based delivery.

Community focus
