How to read this report. Current stock is counted in physical sale units. “Verified units” and gross item sales are non-cancelled orders with payment verified during orders placed in the selected period; requested kg is the customer's combined size on verified lines. Gross item sales are before recorded refunds and exclude delivery fees. Unverified orders reserve stock but are not counted as sales. Cancelled orders are excluded and their stock is restored.
