How these figures are calculated

Gross revenue: every order that was not cancelled (items and delivery fees, after discounts).

Net revenue: orders marked Delivered or Picked up, excluding delivery fees.

Delivery fees collected: fees on non-cancelled orders whose payment has been verified.

Cancelled value: total value of orders marked Cancelled.
